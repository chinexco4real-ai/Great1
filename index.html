The path every student takes

From first question to finished project.

Every Eduvixa course, from AI Explorers to Build Your First AI App, moves through the same four stages.

01

Discover

Take a free placement class, find the right track for your age and interest, and meet your mentor.

02

Learn

Join small live sessions, twice a week, where mentors teach concepts through demos, not lectures.

03

Build

Apply every concept to a hands-on AI project chosen by the student — not a worksheet, a real build.

04

Showcase

Present the finished project at a live demo day and earn a certificate and portfolio piece.

Why Eduvixa

Built for how young people actually learn.

Not a video library. Not a chatbot tutor. A real classroom, run by real people, built around real projects.

Real human mentors

Every class is led by a practitioner or educator — not a pre-recorded video, not an AI chatbot.

Small live cohorts

Twelve students per class, max. Every kid gets to ask questions, build, and be heard.

Project-first curriculum

No lecture without a build. Every unit ends in something the student actually made.

Kid-safe by design

Moderated platforms, background-checked mentors, and a live parent dashboard for every account.

Certificates & portfolio

A shareable project portfolio and certificate for every course a student completes.

Flexible pacing

Live cohorts or self-paced tracks, so learning fits around school rather than competing with it.
